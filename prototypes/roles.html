Roles & permissions

Roles bundle permissions. Assign a role to a user to grant everything it allows.

New role
Role Description Members Type Actions
Owner Full, unrestricted access Every permission in every module. Cannot be edited or removed. 1 System
Administrator Configure the workspace Manages users, roles, organizations, and every business module across the workspace. 3 System
Warehouse Manager Inventory and receiving Full inventory, stock movements, transfers, and goods receiving. Read-only elsewhere. 5 Custom
Cashier Point of Sale checkout Runs POS sales and returns, reads product and price data. No pricing or catalog edits. 12 Custom
Accountant Finance and reporting Journals, receivables, payables, payments, and financial reports. Approves invoices. 2 Custom
Sales Rep Quotations and orders Creates quotations and sales orders, manages customers. Cannot post invoices or refunds. 8 Custom
Viewer Read-only across modules Sees dashboards, records, and reports in every module. Creates or changes nothing. 4 System
